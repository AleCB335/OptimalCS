# A New Optimization Model for the Siting and Sizing of Synchronous Condensers with Discrete Ratings in Weak Grids
High penetration of inverter-based resources reduces the short-circuit strength of transmission networks and
compromises the secure operation of converter stations. This paper extends a sensitivity-based methodology for siting and sizing
synchronous condensers by incorporating discrete equipment ratings and an investment cost function estimated from market
data. The problem is formulated as an integer optimization model whose binary variables select, at each candidate bus,
one rating from a discrete set that includes the option of no installation. The model was solved in Python and applied to the
68-bus NETS–NYPS test system, in which the NETS subsystem is supplied entirely by inverter-based generation. The solution
installs 650 MVAr of buildable ratings at three buses, including the two locations selected by the reference methodology, with an
investment cost of 27.75 MUSD, 7.1 percent below that of the exhaustive discrete evaluation of the reference.
